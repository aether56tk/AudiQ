Research question

Can routine audiometric information identify speech-in-noise performance that is unexpectedly poor relative to the person's audiogram?

This is a research data-collection and analysis tool. It is not a diagnostic device and does not provide clinical predictions or treatment recommendations.

The prototype deliberately does not invent an expected SIN score. After enough cases are collected, the dataset can be exported to Python/scikit-learn and a held-out model can estimate expected performance. Mismatch = observed outcome − model-predicted outcome, with direction interpreted according to the chosen SIN metric.
