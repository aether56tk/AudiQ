# Security Policy

AudiQ is an educational and supervised clinical-training utility. Do not enter identifiable patient information into the public deployment or repository.

Do not publish credentials, private data, or exploitable details in public issues. Contact the maintainer privately through the GitHub profile.

Relevant reports include unsafe audiogram-image handling, XSS or unsafe DOM/file processing, privacy or local-storage exposure, dependency/workflow vulnerabilities, and unsafe imported-data handling.

AudiQ does not bypass device authentication or replace calibrated audiometric equipment.
