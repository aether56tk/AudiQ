Annotation rules

  • Annotate the symbol, not the connecting line.
  • Keep one annotation per visible symbol.
  • Use OTHER when the mark cannot be confidently classified.
  • Do not place names, IDs, DOBs or other direct identifiers in exported data.
  • All annotations require human verification before training.
