Browser-based annotation tool for the AudiQ computer-vision dataset. Images remain local until you export.

2. Annotate

Click the center of each audiogram symbol. The box is generated automatically. Enter clinically verified frequency/dB metadata; the annotation tool does not infer clinical values.

3. Review annotations

Annotation rules

  • Annotate the symbol, not the connecting line.
  • Keep one annotation per visible symbol.
  • Use OTHER when a mark cannot be confidently classified.
  • Keep masked status explicit when known.
  • Do not place names, IDs, DOBs or other direct identifiers in annotations.
  • All annotations require human verification before training.
